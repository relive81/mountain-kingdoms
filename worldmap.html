Six valleys, nine passes, one peak

Mountain ridges split the realm into six valleys around King's Peak. The only ways between them are the passes, and whoever holds a pass decides who gets through. Tap a pass, switch the layers, and send a march.

Legend

Why passes are the heart of the game: you cannot simply teleport across a ridge. Every march between valleys goes through a pass, so alliances fight to hold them. The owner of a pass can charge a toll on foreign marches, sees everyone who comes through, and its Pass Guardians get their terrain bond there.

Valleys and the peak: new players start in the outer valleys. Each valley is controlled by the alliance that holds most of its passes. At the centre stands King's Peak, reachable only through three Royal Passes that open during the season war, the server's endgame.

What lives on the map: player castles, resource tiles (wood, stone, food, iron), goblin camps, and the events: the Storm Portal and the Div of the Pass appear right on the passes.
