A rally against the Storm Golem

Watch an alliance rally form, march through the pass and take on the Storm Golem and the goblins guarding its portal. The battle plays automatically, as it would in the game.

What you are seeing: a rally leader opens a rally and alliance members join with their troops. The rally is led by the Vishap and three heroes, one per troop type, as in the march structure from the design document.

The battle: archers fire from the back, cavalry charges first and infantry follows. Goblins pour out of the portal to protect the Golem, and the Golem slams the front line. At the right moment the Vishap uses Pass Freeze: it freezes the enemy and deals a huge hit to the Golem.

After the battle: wounded troops go to the infirmary rather than dying, and the rally's damage counts toward the event ranking and the rewards.
