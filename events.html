Mountain Kingdoms · Alliance events

Two alliance events, every 48 hours

Our answer to Bear Trap and the portal events: two rally events where the alliance deals as much damage as it can. They alternate, so there is something to do every day, and each one resets every 48 hours. Both are playable below.

The alliance leader picks the start time, so it suits most members' time zone.

    How it works in the game: every 48 hours the Div comes down to your alliance's mountain pass for a 30-minute window. Members send rallies, and the damage of the whole alliance opens shared chests, plus personal rewards by rank.

    Why Guardians matter here: the fight is on a pass tile, so every Guardian's terrain bond is active (+25% in this demo). The Div's weakness rotates each cycle, and a Guardian that matches it hits 1.5× harder. That gives a reason to raise all four Guardians.

    Long-term goal: beat the Div completely and next cycle he comes back one rank higher, with better chests.

    Your rally

    Damage ranking

      How it works in the game: every 48 hours a storm portal opens on a mountain pass. For 30 minutes, players start rallies on it and alliance members join them, just like Bear Trap. The goal is maximum damage: rewards go by personal damage and by alliance ranking.

      The twist that makes it ours: the portal breathes. It shields itself (damage ×0.5), then the rift opens (damage ×2). A rally takes time to march, so a good rally leader times the launch to land in the open rift, and sometimes launches before the rally is full to catch it.

      Join phase: after you start a rally, members join for a short window (5 minutes in the game, 8 seconds here). Wait for a full rally, or launch early with "Launch now".
